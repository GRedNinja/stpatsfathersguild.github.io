Our Mission

St. Patrick’s Fathers Guild is a charitable organization dedicated to supporting fathers and grandfathers who are welcoming a new child into their family. Through prayer and care packages, we help men embrace their vocation with strength and devotion.

The Father’s & Grandfather’s Pledge

Before receiving a care package, each man makes a simple but powerful pledge:

“I pledge to be the best father or grandfather I can be, to protect, guide, and love the children entrusted to me, and to seek God’s grace daily in fulfilling this vocation.”

After making the pledge, they are enrolled in our Prayer Registry and receive a care package prepared by our volunteers.

Fatherhood Attestation Form

Please complete this brief, non-invasive attestation to receive your care package.









“I affirm that I am an expecting father or grandfather, and I request enrollment in the Prayer Registry and a care package from St. Patrick’s Fathers Guild.”



Prayer Registry

All fathers and grandfathers who make the pledge are placed on our perpetual prayer list. Donors may also request to be added to the registry.

Our guild prays weekly for:

  • Expectant fathers and grandfathers
  • Families welcoming new children
  • Donors who support our mission

Support the Guild

Donations help us assemble care packages, maintain the prayer registry, and expand our outreach. Every donor may request inclusion in the prayer registry.
